Delivery

We deliver inside Nigeria only. Orders are confirmed by telephone before anything is dispatched, so the address and the delivery window are agreed with you first.

    If nobody is available

    The rider will try to reach you on the number you gave when ordering. If the delivery cannot be completed, we will call you to arrange another attempt. Repeated failed attempts may mean the order is cancelled, and we will tell you if that happens rather than leave you guessing.

    Checking your parcel

    Open the parcel and look at what is inside before you pay the rider. If the product is not what you ordered, or the pack is damaged or already opened, do not pay for it. Call us straight away.

    Returns and refunds

    How to start a return

    Contact us first, with your order reference, before sending anything back. A return that arrives without us knowing about it is very hard for us to match to an order, which only delays your refund.

    What we cannot do

    We cannot offer a refund because a supplement did not produce a particular result. No seller can honestly promise you a specific outcome from a food supplement, and we are not going to be the ones who pretend otherwise. If you are unwell or something is worrying you, please speak to a doctor or a pharmacist.

    Privacy

    What we collect

    Only what is needed to deliver an order: your name, your phone number or numbers, your state, your city or LGA, your delivery address, an optional landmark, and any delivery note you choose to add.

    What we do not collect

    We do not ask for, and you should never send us, any information about your health, your sexual history, your fertility, or any medical condition. We do not ask for card numbers, bank details, a PIN or a one-time code, and nobody from our team will ever ask you for one. We do not require you to create an account.

    How your details are used

    • To call you and confirm your order.
    • To pass the delivery address to the rider who brings your parcel.
    • To answer you if you contact us about that order.

    If you ticked the optional marketing box, we may also message you about offers. If you did not tick it, we will not. You can ask us to stop at any time and we will.

    Who else sees it

    The delivery company handling your parcel sees the name, number and address needed to deliver it. Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade customer details.

    This website

    This site runs no advertising pixels and no third-party tracking. Basic counts of which sections are viewed are kept in your own browser's storage and are never transmitted anywhere. Your order details are held in your browser only long enough to show you the confirmation page, then cleared.

    Your rights

    Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it once your order is complete and any return window has passed. Contact us and we will deal with it.
